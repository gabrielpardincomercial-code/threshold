When it runs

What happens

We leave the studio at nine and run eight easy kilometres along the harbour and back. Two pace groups, both conversational, both with a coach at the back so nobody runs alone. Heart-rate straps go on as usual and the numbers are on the wall when you return.

Who it is for

Runners, almost-runners and lifters who have been told to do some cardio. If you can run five kilometres without stopping, you can do this.

What you will notice

  • Easy pace getting quicker without trying.

  • Saturdays that start well.

  • You will know the best flat white on the water.
