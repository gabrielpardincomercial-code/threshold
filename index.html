About the studio

We coach the number on the wall, not the one on the scale.

Thres opened in a tannery warehouse in 2019 with twelve chest straps and one idea: train at the right effort and the rest follows. Six years on, the straps have multiplied. The idea has not changed.

Do I need my own heart-rate monitor?

No. We lend you a chest strap for every class and it pairs with the screens in the room. If you already own a strap or a watch that broadcasts heart rate, bring it.

I'm not fit yet. Will I keep up?

Zones are personal. Your Zone 4 and your neighbour's Zone 4 feel the same and look completely different, which is the point. Nobody is behind.

How many classes a week should I do?

Most members settle on three: one easy, one steady, one hard. Your coach will help you choose the days in your first week.

What happens in my first class?

Arrive fifteen minutes early. We fit your strap, find your maximum heart rate with a short ramp test and walk you round the room. The class itself is on us.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. Pause for up to two months a year, or cancel with thirty days' notice, both from your account. No forms and no phone calls.

Where are you, and is there parking?

Unit 4, Tannery Wharf, five minutes on foot from the station. There are bike racks at the door and a public car park across the bridge.
